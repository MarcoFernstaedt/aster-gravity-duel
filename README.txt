# Gravity Duel

A tiny offline arcade game made for Marco by Aster.

## Play

1. Open `gravity-duel.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No install, account or internet is needed.
2. Choose **Solo vs AI** or **2 Player Local**. Two-player mode is turn-based on the same screen; send this ZIP to a friend if they want their own copy.
3. Move the pointer over the arena to aim and click to fire. On a phone, tap the arena to aim and fire. Adjust the **Power** slider before shooting.
4. Keyboard: left/right arrows rotate aim, up/down arrows change power, Space or Enter fires. The first pilot to break the opponent's three shields wins.
5. `Menu` resets the match. `Sound` toggles the synthesised game tones.

The gravity wells move. Purple attracts your pulse, amber pushes it away. Use the dotted preview and bank shots off the walls and bumpers.

This is **local multiplayer**, not an online server. The file does not request network resources or collect account data.
